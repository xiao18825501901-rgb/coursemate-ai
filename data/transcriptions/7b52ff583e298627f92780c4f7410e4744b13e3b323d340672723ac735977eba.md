K-means clustering is useful in computer and engineering fields. For instance, in computer graphics, K-means clustering could be used to reduce the number of different colors in a stored image. In this question, we consider a color image that initially contains 16 different colors. Each used color is represented as a tuple of 3 numbers in the range 0–255 for its Red, Green and Blue components respectively. Zero represents the total absence of a color, while 255 represents 100% of that color. The 16 used colors are tabulated above.
